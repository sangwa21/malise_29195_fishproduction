### 2. Purpose of Research

The purpose of this research is to understand fish production and aquaculture in Rwanda, examine existing fisheries monitoring systems, identify suitable datasets, and explore the technologies required to develop a Fish Production and Sustainability Monitoring System.

The collected sources will support the project's database design, data analysis, implementation, and interactive dashboard development.

## Source 2: Research Paper – Aquaculture in Rwanda

**Title:** Aquaculture and aquafeed in Rwanda: Current status and perspectives

**Authors:** Niyibizi, L., Vidakovic, A., Norman Haldén, A., Rukera Tabaro, S., & Lundh, T.

**Year:** 2023

**Journal:** Journal of Applied Aquaculture, 35(3), 743–764.

**Summary:**

This study examines aquaculture practices in Rwanda, including information collected from 67 fish farms. It discusses commonly farmed fish species, including Nile tilapia, common carp, and North African catfish. It also examines fish farming methods and locally available feed ingredients.

**Relevance to the Project:**

This research will help identify fish species and aquaculture practices that can be considered when designing the project's database. It also provides background information about fish farming in Rwanda.

**APA Reference:**

Niyibizi, L., Vidakovic, A., Norman Haldén, A., Rukera Tabaro, S., & Lundh, T. (2023). Aquaculture and aquafeed in Rwanda: Current status and perspectives. *Journal of Applied Aquaculture, 35*(3), 743–764. https://doi.org/10.1080/10454438.2021.2024315

**Source Link:**

## Source 3: Existing Fisheries Monitoring System

**System Name:** PeskAAS (Peskas)

**Organization:** WorldFish and research partners

**Year:** 2020

**Description:**

PeskAAS is an open-source digital fisheries monitoring and analytics system developed and tested in Timor-Leste. It collects, processes, analyzes, and presents fisheries catch information through an interactive dashboard.

**Relevance to the Project:**

This system provides an example of how fisheries information can be collected, organized, analyzed, and displayed. Its monitoring and dashboard features will provide ideas for developing the interactive dashboard planned for this project.

**APA Reference:**

Tilley, A., Dos Reis Lopes, J., & Wilkinson, S. P. (2020). PeskAAS: A near-real-time, open-source monitoring and analytics system for small-scale fisheries. *PLOS ONE, 15*(11), e0234760. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0234760

**Source Link:**

https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0234760

## Source 4: Dataset – FAO Fishery and Aquaculture Statistics

**Dataset Name:** FAO Fishery and Aquaculture Statistics (FishStat)

**Organization:** Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)

**Year:** 2026 (March 2026 release; data covering 1950–2024)

**Description:**

FAO FishStat provides international statistics on aquaculture and capture fisheries production. The data includes production quantities organized by country, species, and year.

The project will use the relevant fisheries and aquaculture production records to examine production trends and compare different production categories. The wider dataset will also be retained where necessary to support meaningful data analysis.

**Data License and Attribution:**

The applicable FAO dataset licence and attribution requirements must be checked against the downloaded dataset and its accompanying terms before redistribution. The dataset's stated CC BY 4.0 terms should be followed where applicable.

**Relevance to the Project:**

This dataset will provide real-world production information for the project. It will support analysis of fish production trends, comparisons between aquaculture and capture fisheries, and the generation of dashboard summaries.

**APA Reference:**

Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations. (2026). *Global fisheries and aquaculture production statistics (FishStat).* https://www.fao.org/statistics/data-collection/fishery-and-aquaculture/en

**Source Link:**

https://www.fao.org/statistics/data-collection/fishery-and-aquaculture/en

**Data Access:**

The dataset will be downloaded from the official FAO statistics platform. The number of downloaded records and the applicable licence will be documented during data preparation.

## Source 5: Research Paper – Fish Species Diversity in Rwanda

**Title:** Current status of Cyprinids abundance and diversity and their potential for aquaculture production in Rwanda

**Authors:** Mwimba, R., Rukera Tabaro, S., & Syaghalirwa, N. M. M.

**Year:** 2024

**Journal:** Rwanda Journal of Agricultural Sciences, 3(1), 20–37.

This research investigates the abundance and diversity of Cyprinid fish species in Rwandan water bodies. It examines fish species diversity and their potential contribution to aquaculture production.
